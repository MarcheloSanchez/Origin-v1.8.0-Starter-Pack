# Day 1: The Walk That Started Everything

[[00-Tutorial-Guide|← Back to Guide]] | [[02-Day-Two|Day 2 →]]

## Story Context

It's a crisp Sunday morning. Alex decides to take a walk through Kew Gardens to clear their mind. The late-winter sun filters through bare branches as they wander among ancient oaks.

Near a particularly old tree, Alex notices something fascinating - a network of mushrooms emerging from the soil, connecting multiple trees. A sign nearby explains this is the "Wood Wide Web" - how trees communicate through underground fungal networks.

*"Wait,"* Alex thinks, *"this is exactly like how a good PKM system should work..."*

---

## The Capture Moment

**8:45 AM - Standing among the trees**

Alex pulls out their phone and uses Origin's quick capture:

> "Trees talk through underground networks - mushroom roots connect them all. Like a forest internet. Reminds me of how PKM systems connect ideas..."

**Hotkey used**: `Ctrl+Shift+I` (Quick Inbox Capture)

## Processing (That Evening)

### Step 1: Open the Inbox
Alex opens `+Inbox` folder and sees the raw capture waiting.

### Step 2: Ask the GTD Question
*"What is this?"*
- Not actionable → It's **knowledge/insight**
- Goes to → **Atomics** (02-Dots/100-Atomics)

### Step 3: Apply the Atomic Template
Alex creates a proper atomic note using the template:
- Adds a one-liner summary
- Tags it appropriately
- Sets maturity to `📤seed` (just starting)

### Step 4: Create Supporting Notes
- **Place note** for Kew Gardens (where the insight happened)
- **Daily note** to log the day
