- Influences thinking beyond PKM
- Becomes a reference for others

## Archiving Completed Work

### What Gets Archived?

1. **Processed inbox items** - Raw captures that became atomic notes
2. **Completed project phases** - Phase 1 of Master Origin Vault
3. **Obsolete drafts** - Earlier versions replaced by better ones

### The Archive Process

```
Note Status: ✅completed
        ↓
Add archive metadata:
- archive_reason: completed
- original_location: [path]
- archived_date: 2026-02-02
        ↓
Move to: 06-Archive/
        ↓
Tag: #📦archived
```

- Always add archive_reason metadata
- Maintain original structure in archive
- Review archived items quarterly
