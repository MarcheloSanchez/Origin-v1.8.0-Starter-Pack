## Building the MOC

### What is a MOC?

A **Map of Content** is a note that:
- Navigates a topic area
- Links related notes together
- Provides context and synthesis
- Grows as you add more notes

It's not a folder - it's a **thinking space**.

### The Growth Patterns MOC

Alex creates a MOC called "Growth Patterns" to unite the week's discoveries:

```markdown
# MOC - Growth Patterns

## Abstract
Growth patterns describe how systems, networks, and knowledge
evolve over time. Small consistent actions create disproportionate
results. Connections between elements create value greater than
the sum of parts.

## Core Notes
- [[Mycelium Networks]] - Biological model
- [[Compound Growth]] - Mathematical principle
- [[Network Effects]] - Value multiplication
- [[Systems Thinking]] - Theoretical framework

## The Synthesis
Whether in forests, knowledge systems, or relationships:
1. Connections create resilience
2. Small additions compound
3. Emergence happens at critical mass
```

## MOC Structure Template

```markdown
# MOC - [Topic Name]

## Abstract
What this topic is about and why it matters.
2-3 sentences of synthesis.

## Core Subtopics
### [Subtopic 1]
- [[Note 1]]
- [[Note 2]]

### [Subtopic 2]
- [[Note 3]]
- [[Note 4]]

## Anchors (Start Here)
The best entry points for this topic.

## The Synthesis
Your unique insight that connects everything.
What emerges from the combination of these notes?

## Development Pipeline
### 🌱 Growing (Need Development)
- Topics to explore further

### 🌲 Evergreen (Well-Developed)
- Stable, mature notes
```
