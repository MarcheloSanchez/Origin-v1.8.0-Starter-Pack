## Story Context

Tuesday afternoon. Alex is working at their favorite coffee shop when they overhear someone at the next table talking about... forest communication networks?

Alex introduces themselves, and meets **Dr. Maya Chen**, a forest ecologist from Cambridge researching exactly what Alex observed at Kew Gardens.

*"You saw the mycelium network!"* Maya exclaims. *"That's my life's work - proving that forests are intelligent, interconnected systems."*

---

## The Conversation

### Key Points Discussed

1. **Mother Trees**: Older trees recognize their offspring and send them extra nutrients
2. **Warning Systems**: Trees can signal danger to others through the network
3. **Network Resilience**: Removing key trees damages the entire ecosystem
4. **Research Paper**: Maya offers to send her latest paper on forest networks

### Action Items Captured

- [ ] Maya will send her paper via email
- [ ] Alex will read it and share thoughts
- [ ] Potential future meeting to discuss PKM parallels
