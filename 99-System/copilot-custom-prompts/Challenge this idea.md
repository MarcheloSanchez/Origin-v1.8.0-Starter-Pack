Act as a devil's advocate for {}. Challenge this content by:

1. **Counter-arguments**: What are the strongest objections?
2. **Missing perspectives**: What viewpoints are absent?
3. **Assumptions**: What unstated assumptions exist?
4. **Edge cases**: Where might this break down?
5. **Alternative interpretations**: How else could this be read?

Be constructive but rigorous. The goal is to strengthen the idea, not destroy it.
