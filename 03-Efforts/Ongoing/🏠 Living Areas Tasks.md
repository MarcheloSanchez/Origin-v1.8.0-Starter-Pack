# 🛋️ Living Areas Tasks

> [!tip]+ **Quick Reference**
> **Why**: Living/social space impacts daily mood and hospitality confidence. Quick cleaning prevents dust accumulation
> **When**: Daily tidy, weekly deep on Thursday, monthly focus rotation
> **Time**: Daily (5 min) + Weekly (8 min) + Monthly (20-25 min)

---

## ✅ DAILY ROUTINE (5 min)

**Evening / Throughout day:**
- [ ] Plump couch cushions
- [ ] Put items back on shelves/in place
- [ ] Toss any trash/dishes
- [ ] Straighten throw blankets
- [ ] Clear coffee table
- [ ] Quick sweep if visible debris

**Why daily**: Living areas get constant use. Quick reset prevents overwhelm.

---

## 📅 WEEKLY ROUTINE - Thursday (8 min)

**"Living Room Refresh Thursday":**
- [ ] Dust all surfaces (tables, shelves, TV stand, baseboards)
- [ ] Vacuum thoroughly (under cushions, corners)
- [ ] Clean mirrors/glass surfaces (fingerprints)
- [ ] Wipe light switches & remote controls
- [ ] Straighten any organized items (books, remotes, etc.)
- [ ] Fluff cushions, fold throws neatly

**Timing**: 8 minutes with consistent daily maintenance

---

## 🔍 MONTHLY DEEP-CLEAN (20-25 min) - Living Area Focus Month

**Do this ONCE per month when it's "Living Room Week"**

### Furniture Deep Clean (8 min)
- [ ] Vacuum under couch/chairs (move furniture if possible)
- [ ] Wipe down couch arms & legs
- [ ] Flip or rotate couch cushions
- [ ] Check for and remove any items under furniture
- [ ] Dust picture frames on walls

### Surfaces & Fixtures (8 min)
- [ ] Dust ceiling fan/light fixtures thoroughly
- [ ] Wipe all window frames & sills
- [ ] Clean light switches & doorknobs
- [ ] Wipe thermostat
- [ ] Dust baseboards completely
- [ ] Wipe any shelving inside/out

### Floor & Details (5 min)
- [ ] Vacuum thoroughly including corners
- [ ] Wipe down door frames
- [ ] Check under couch for forgotten items
- [ ] Reorganize throw blankets/pillows

### Refresh (2 min)
- [ ] Light candle or spray room refresher
- [ ] Fluff and arrange décor items
- [ ] Step back and appreciate the space

---

## 🎯 Living Area Success Standards

**Daily-Check Level**: "Tidy and welcoming"
- Furniture straightened
- Floor clear
- No visible clutter on surfaces
- Inviting to enter

**Weekly Level**: "Dust-free and fresh"
- All surfaces dusted
- Floor vacuumed
- Mirrors gleam
- Organized appearance
- Comfortable for guests

**Monthly Level**: "Completely refreshed"
- Deep under-furniture clean
- Furniture rearranged or rotated (optional)
- All details shine
- Light, airy feeling
- Guest-ready

---

## 💡 Pro Tips for Efficiency

| Challenge | Solution |
|-----------|----------|
| "Dust collects fast" | Weekly dust prevents thick buildup; easier each time |
| "Clutter accumulates" | Daily 5-min reset prevents overwhelming pile-up |
| "Under furniture gathers dust bunnies" | Monthly move of furniture solves this completely |
| "Coffee table becomes catch-all" | Rule: Only items in use stay on surface |
| "Cushions look squashed" | Flip and rotate monthly; plump daily (3 second habit) |
| "Dark corners accumulate cobwebs" | Monthly check finds them before they spread |
| "Remote/keys always missing" | Designated basket on table; check daily |

## 👥 Hospitality Readiness

Want to have people over spontaneously?
- **Daily routine done**: Immediately guest-ready (10/10)
- **Daily + weekly done**: Perfectly comfortable (9/10)
- **All three done**: Showcase ready (10/10)

This system enables spontaneity instead of "I need to clean first."

## 🔄 Routine Variations

**If super busy this week?**
- Daily only (clear surfaces, tidy cushions)
- Skip weekly, do next week

**If have extra time?**
- Deep dust high shelves
- Organize media/remotes
- Rearrange furniture

**If hosting guests soon?**
- Do weekly + monthly in one session (20 min)
- Creates fresh, welcoming space

---

## 🎨 Décor Maintenance Notes

If you have art/plants/décor:
- Dust picture frames weekly (5 sec)
- Check plants weekly (water, dust leaves)
- Rotate décor quarterly to refresh feeling
- Note anything needing repair for seasonal projects
