## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Mother Trees
Maya explained that older trees in a forest recognize their offspring and send them extra nutrients through the mycelium network. "It's like they're nurturing their children underground."

### 2. Warning Systems
Trees can signal danger to other trees through the network. When one tree is attacked by pests, it sends chemical signals through the mycelium that reach other trees faster than airborne chemicals.

### 3. Network Resilience
When logging removes key "hub" trees, the entire network suffers - not just the immediate area. Maya's research shows ecosystem health correlates with network connectivity.

### 4. PKM Parallels
Maya was fascinated by the parallel I drew to knowledge management. She'd never thought of PKM as a network system but immediately saw the connection.
