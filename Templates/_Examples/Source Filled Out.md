# Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte

## 📝 TL;DR — 5 bullets
1. **CODE Method**: Capture, Organize, Distill, Express - systematic approach to knowledge work
2. **Progressive Summarization**: Layer highlighting and distillation to extract key insights
3. **PARA System**: Projects, Areas, Resources, Archives for organizing digital information
4. **Just-in-Time Organization**: Organize when you need it, not preemptively
5. **Creative Output Focus**: Knowledge system should enable creation, not just consumption

## 💡 Key Insights
1. **"Your brain is for having ideas, not storing them"** - External systems free mental capacity for creative thinking
2. **The Archipelago of Ideas** - Connect scattered information to form new understanding
3. **Start with abundance** - Capture more than you think you need, filter later
4. **Intermediate Packets** - Create reusable units of work that compound over time
5. **Diverge then converge** - Separate expansion and compression phases in knowledge work
