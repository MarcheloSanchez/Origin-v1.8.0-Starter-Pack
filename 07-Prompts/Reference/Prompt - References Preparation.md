## 📋Instructions 
```ENG
You are an expert job search consultant. I am facing [describe problem in detail with context]. I need to prepare a list of professional references for my job application. Can you help me format and write a brief introduction for each reference that highlights their relationship to me and their relevance to the position? Provide clear and professional examples.  
```

Můžete mi pomoci naformátovat a napsat stručný úvod pro každou referenci, který zdůrazní jejich vztah ke mně a jejich význam pro danou pozici? Uveďte jasné a profesionální příklady.
