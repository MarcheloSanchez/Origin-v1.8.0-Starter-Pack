## 📋Instructions 
```ENG
I run a business in the [your industry] sector, focusing on [brief description of your business].
We want to make our online content more varied and interesting to better connect With our
audience and boost our online presence. We're looking to create [articles/youtube scripts,
etc].
I need your help to come up with 30 creative and relevant content ideas that fit our industry and really appeal to our target audience, Who are [describe your audience].

List these ideas in a table With columns for 'Suggest d Topicí, 'Brief Description', and '3 Possible Titles'. Under 'Brief Description', give a short summary or special twist for each topic. The '3 Possible Titles' should be attention-grabbing and show different ways to approach the topic. 

Your ideas are crucial for helping us make content that really speaks to our audience and helps our brand stand out online.
```

```CZ
Podnikám v odvětví [váš obor] se zaměřením na [stručný popis vašeho podnikání]. Chceme, aby náš online obsah byl pestřejší a zajímavější, abychom lépe navázali kontakt s naším publikem a posílili naši online prezentaci. Chceme vytvořit [články/skripty na YouTube, atd]. Potřebuji, abyste mi pomohli vymyslet 30 kreativních a relevantních nápadů na obsah, který by se hodil do našeho oboru a skutečně oslovil naši cílovou skupinu, Která je [popište své publikum]. Vypište tyto nápady do tabulky se sloupci "Navrhněte d Téma", "Stručný popis" a "3 možné názvy". V části "Stručný popis" uveďte u každého tématu krátké shrnutí nebo zvláštní odbočku. V části "3 možné názvy" by měly upoutat pozornost a ukázat různé způsoby, jak k tématu přistupovat. Vaše nápady jsou klíčové, protože nám pomohou vytvořit obsah, který skutečně osloví naše publikum a pomůže naší značce vyniknout na internetu.
```

### V2 
## Outline for xyz
```
I need to make a detailed and unique outline for an [article/script/etc.] about '[your niche]'. The topic is X, and the title is Y.
This is for [describe your audience].

In the outline, please include:

Introduction: Write an engaging start that introduces the topic and suits our audience's level
of understanding.

Key Points: Note down the main ideas or parts of the content:
-A quick summary of each point.
-Detailed parts or topics under each point.
-Questions or views to consider, based on What our audience likes.
-Suggestions for real examples or case studies that match our audience's interests and add weight to each point.
-Visuals and Media (if needed): Ideas for pictures or videos that our audience would like, and how they should be presented.

Conclusion: End it in a way that sums up the main points and leaves the audience thinking or inspires them to act.

Audience Engagement: Ways to make the content more interactive or interesting for our audience.

Additional Notes: Any special ideas, writing styles, or storytelling methods that would make the content more engaging for our audience.
```
