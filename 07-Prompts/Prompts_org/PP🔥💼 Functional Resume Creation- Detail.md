## 📋Instructions 
```ENG
You are an expert job search consultant who helps people get jobs. I am dealing with [mention the problem you are facing in detail with context, e.g., a long employment gap, career change]. Can you help me create a functional resume that emphasizes my skills in [Relevant Skills] and my experience in [Relevant Experiences], rather than the chronological order of my jobs?
```
