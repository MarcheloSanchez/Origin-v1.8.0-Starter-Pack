# 🚪 Entry & Laundry Tasks

> [!tip]+ **Quick Reference**
> **Why**: Entry is first impression. Laundry prevents overflowing piles and keeps clean clothes accessible
> **When**: Entry checked daily, laundry routine varies, both support overall functioning
> **Time**: Daily entry (2 min) + Laundry flexible + Weekly deep (5 min)

---

## ✅ DAILY ROUTINE

### Entry (2 min)
- [ ] Remove shoes, place in designated spot
- [ ] Hang coat/jacket properly
- [ ] Toss mail into bin (not on counter)
- [ ] Check for items that don't belong
- [ ] Spot-sweep if tracked mud/dirt

### Laundry Check (1 min)
- [ ] Check if laundry machine/dryer needs attention
- [ ] Move wet clothes to dryer if needed
- [ ] Put dry clothes in basket for folding

**Why daily**: Laundry left in washer = mildew. Quick entry tidy = mental clarity.

---

## 🧺 LAUNDRY ROUTINE (Flexible - Adapt to Your Schedule)

### Option 1: Twice Weekly (Most Manageable)
- **Monday or Tuesday**: Large load (sheets, towels, or heavy items)
- **Thursday or Friday**: Medium load (regular clothes)

**Process**:
1. Start wash in morning (5 min setup)
2. Move to dryer after work (2 min)
3. Fold & put away evening (10-15 min)

### Option 2: Weekly (Larger Load)
- **Saturday or Sunday**: All laundry (clothes + sheets + towels)

**Timing**: 30-40 min total (spread across day)

### Option 3: As-Needed (Minimal)
- Do wash when hamper is full or running low on underwear
- Minimal mental load but requires flexibility

**Choose what works for your lifestyle** - consistency matters more than schedule.

---

## 📅 WEEKLY ROUTINE - Friday (5 min)

**"Entry & Laundry Refresh Friday":**
- [ ] Sweep entry thoroughly (corners, under rug)
- [ ] Wipe entry light switch
- [ ] Check coat closet organization
- [ ] Organize shoes/boots in space
- [ ] Wipe down entry table/console
- [ ] Check laundry hamper level (plan wash if needed)

---

## 🔍 MONTHLY DEEP-CLEAN (10-15 min) - Entry Focus Month

**Do this ONCE per month when it's "Entry Week"**

### Entry Deep Clean (8 min)
- [ ] Sweep & mop entry floor thoroughly
- [ ] Wipe all door surfaces (inside & out if accessible)
- [ ] Clean window near door (if applicable)
- [ ] Wipe baseboards
- [ ] Organize coat closet completely (remove unused items)
- [ ] Check for cobwebs near entrance

### Laundry Area Organization (5 min)
- [ ] Wipe down washer/dryer exterior
- [ ] Clean inside dryer lint trap area (remove lint)
- [ ] Organize laundry detergent/supplies
- [ ] Check for spills or messes in laundry area
- [ ] Ensure hamper/baskets are functional

### Maintenance Check (2 min)
- [ ] Check door hinges/locks for wear
- [ ] Note any needed repairs (squeaky hinge, etc.)
- [ ] Verify entry rug is secure (no trip hazard)

---

## 🎯 Entry & Laundry Success Standards

**Daily-Check Level**: "Functional and organized"
- Shoes put away
- Coats hung
- No chaos by door
- Laundry not stuck in washer

**Weekly Level**: "Clean entrance"
- Entry swept clean
- Shoes organized
- Light switch clean
- No clutter visible

**Monthly Level**: "Professional ready"
- Entry sparkling
- Coat closet organized
- Laundry area tidy
- No mildew smells

---

## 💡 Pro Tips for Efficiency

| Challenge | Solution |
|-----------|----------|
| "Laundry piles up" | Start wash in morning, move to dryer after work = auto |
| "Clothes in washer get moldy" | Set 2-hour phone reminder to move to dryer |
| "Folding takes forever" | Fold & put away same day (wet clothes are easier to fold) |
| "Entry is catch-all" | Dedicated spots: shoes bin, coat hooks, mail basket |
| "Hamper overflows" | Do laundry when 3/4 full (prevents overflow stress) |
| "Can't find clean socks" | Fold socks into drawer immediately (not in basket) |
| "Lint trap forgot" | Monthly reminder prevents dryer inefficiency + fire risk |

## 🔄 Laundry Routine Variations

**Super busy week?**
- Minimum: One emergency load when out of underwear
- Fold/put away can wait 1-2 days (air dry in basket okay)

**Extra time available?**
- Iron clothes this week
- Deep clean washer (add baking soda, run empty cycle)
- Organize coat closet thoroughly

**Have others in home?**
- Assign laundry days (person X does Monday, person Y does Friday)
- Create folding assembly line
- Store folded clothes on shared basket by bedroom

## 🚪 Entry Logistics

### Shoe Organization
- Maximum shoes in entry: 5-6 pair
- Daily wears accessible; special occasion elsewhere
- Wet/muddy shoes on mat first to dry

### Coat & Jacket System
- Hooks or rack for coats (same number as people)
- Rule: Coat hangs same day it's worn
- Seasonal rotation (heavy coats to storage in spring)

### Mail Management
- Bin or basket for incoming mail
- Weekly sort (Mon or Fri)
- Trash unwanted immediately
- Bills in designated spot

---

## 🧺 Laundry Logistics

### Hamper Strategy
- One hamper per person if household >1 person
- Capacity: 1-2 weeks of clothes
- When 3/4 full = time to wash

### Folding Station
- Designated surface (table, bed, basket)
- Keep laundry detergent nearby
- Basket of items to fold = visual signal

### Storage System
- Socks: Drawer (fold in half, stack neatly)
- Underwear: Drawer (fold once, stand upright)
- Shirts: Fold on shelf or hang on hangers
- Pants: Fold in thirds or hang on hangers
