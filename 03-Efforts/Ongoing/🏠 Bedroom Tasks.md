# 🛏️ Bedroom Tasks

> [!tip]+ **Quick Reference**
> **Why**: Clean, organized bedroom improves sleep quality, reduces stress, supports better rest
> **When**: Daily basics always, weekly deep on Wednesday, monthly deep once/month
> **Time**: Daily (3-5 min) + Weekly (8 min) + Monthly (15-20 min)

---

## ✅ DAILY ROUTINE (3-5 min)

**Morning / Before bed:**
- [ ] Make bed (pulls sheets tight, fluff pillow)
- [ ] Put worn clothes in hamper or back in closet
- [ ] Clear nightstand (only essentials remain)
- [ ] Toss any trash
- [ ] Close closet door if open

**Why daily**: Made bed signals intentionality. Clear room = better sleep. Takes 3 minutes.

---

## 📅 WEEKLY ROUTINE - Wednesday (8 min)

**"Bedroom Refresh Wednesday":**
- [ ] Vacuum floor completely (including under bed if accessible)
- [ ] Dust all surfaces (nightstands, dresser, shelves)
- [ ] Wipe light switches & door handles
- [ ] Straighten closet (hangers all same direction if possible)
- [ ] Check under bed for items that shouldn't be there
- [ ] Spray light room refresher if desired

**Timing**: 8 minutes with preparation

---

## 🔍 MONTHLY DEEP-CLEAN (15-20 min) - Bedroom Focus Month

**Do this ONCE per month when it's "Bedroom Week"**

### Bedding (5 min)
- [ ] Change all sheets (wash old ones)
- [ ] Flip or rotate mattress (extends life, distributes wear)
- [ ] Fluff pillows or air them outside if possible
- [ ] Wash pillow covers

### Closet Organization (8 min)
- [ ] Hang clothes properly (same direction hangers)
- [ ] Fold sweaters/delicates neatly
- [ ] Check floor of closet (any forgotten items?)
- [ ] Organize shoes/accessories
- [ ] Remove hangers from items worn (makes space visible)

### Dust & Refresh (5 min)
- [ ] Dust all surfaces including high spots
- [ ] Wipe baseboards
- [ ] Clean windows/windowsills
- [ ] Wipe mirrors
- [ ] Check corners for cobwebs

### Under-Bed Refresh (2 min)
- [ ] Check what's stored under bed
- [ ] Vacuum/sweep under bed thoroughly
- [ ] Consolidate or remove unnecessary items

---

## 🎯 Bedroom Success Standards

**Daily-Check Level**: "Peaceful to enter"
- Bed made
- Floor clear
- Minimal clutter visible
- Calm, organized feeling

**Weekly Level**: "Clean and cared for"
- Everything dusted
- Floor vacuumed
- Closet organized
- No dirty clothes visible
- Ready for rest

**Monthly Level**: "Refresh complete"
- Fresh sheets on bed
- Closet well-organized
- Mattress flipped
- Deep dust gone
- Space feels renewed

---

## 💡 Pro Tips for Efficiency

| Challenge | Solution |
|-----------|----------|
| "Clothes everywhere" | Designate worn-but-clean pile (specific chair/corner) |
| "Can't find things" | Closet zones: work clothes, casual, sleep, special |
| "Dust accumulates fast" | Weekly dust prevents buildup; takes 3 min |
| "Under-bed becomes storage" | Limit to 2 plastic containers max; check monthly |
| "Closet is overstuffed" | Monthly: remove 2-3 items you don't wear |
| "Can't sleep well" | Clean bed + organized room reduces stress dramatically |
| "Clothes smell stale" | Leave closet door open 15 min after waking for air |

## 😴 Sleep Quality Connection

A clean bedroom directly impacts sleep:
- **Visual clutter** → Brain stress → Harder to fall asleep
- **Dust** → Allergies → Poor sleep quality
- **Made bed** → Psychology of order → Calming effect
- **Fresh sheets** → Comfort → Better sleep depth

## 🔄 Routine Variations

**If super busy this week?**
- Daily only (make bed, clear immediate clutter)
- Skip weekly, do next week

**If have extra time?**
- Organize closet section
- Deep dust high surfaces
- Air out mattress/pillows

**If struggle with motivation?**
- Remember: Made bed + clear floor = 10% sleep quality improvement
- Focus on the 3-minute daily only; weekly can wait
