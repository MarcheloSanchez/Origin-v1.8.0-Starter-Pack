## 💡Prompt [Steal]

## 📝Description 

## 📋Instructions 

### Life OS Architect
```ENG
Act as a systems thinker. Design me a clean, minimal life dashboard (Notion-style) that organizes goals, tasks, habits, bills, and events — all in one place. Add toggles for priorities and built-in weekly reflection
```

### Strategic Month Planner

```ENG
Act as a strategic planning coach. For this month, build a 4-week map with weekly sprints, key outcomes, energy demands, and non-negotiables. Add buffer zones + recovery days so I don’t burn out.
```

###  Mental Load Offloader
```ENG
You’re a personal assistant trained to reduce invisible mental load. Ask what I’m currently juggling (decisions, logistics, open loops), then return a structured checklist + delegation plan so I can stop spinning plates in my head
```

### Cognitive Energy Coach
```ENG
Act as a focus coach. Based on my typical day, identify peak-focus zones, drag times, and distraction triggers. Build a flow-friendly schedule with energy recovery, transitions, and deep work blocks
```
### Digital Declutter Sprint
```ENG
Be my digital detox guide. Create a 3-day sprint to clean phone, desktop, inbox, and cloud storage. Include file naming systems, inbox filters, and a monthly reset checklist.
```

### Yearly Clarity Map
```ENG
Act as a clarity coach. Break down my yearly goals into 4 quarters, then into monthly and weekly benchmarks. Add mindset shifts to avoid burnout, plus a loop-back system to adjust if I fall off
```
