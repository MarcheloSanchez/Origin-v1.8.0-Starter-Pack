# 📝 About Prompts

Welcome to the Prompt Library system, your personalized space for creating, organizing, and maintaining powerful prompts in Obsidian! This system enhances your knowledge management with structured, reusable prompts optimized for discovery and ongoing improvement.

---

## 🎯 Purpose of Prompts

- Provide clear, consistent instructions for various tasks
- Facilitate idea generation, problem solving, and content creation
- Support personal knowledge management (PKM) and workflow automation
- Enable easy search and categorization with metadata and taxonomy

---

## 📚 Structure of a Prompt

Each prompt in this vault follows a consistent format to maximize usability and maintainability:

- **YAML Frontmatter with Attributes**  
  Metadata like unique ID, category, subcategory, intent, difficulty, audience, tone, and lifecycle status  
- **Body Content**  
  - Role and context  
  - Input instructions and constraints  
  - Output expectations and guardrails  
- **Quality Gates and Examples**  
  - Self and peer review checkpoints  
  - Example invocations to demonstrate correct usage  
- **Changelog and Notes**  
  Track modifications and document assumptions

---

## 🗂️ Prompt Categories & Taxonomy

Prompts are organized by clearly defined categories and subcategories for easy browsing and filtering, including but not limited to:

- Writing ✍️: Blog posts, emails, storytelling, copywriting, summaries  
- Coding 💻: Code generation, review, debugging, refactoring, doc comments  
- Business 📊: Strategy, product management, marketing, ops SOPs, analytics  
- Education 🎓: Teaching, assessments, tutoring, study guides  
- Productivity ⚙️: Task checklists, decision support, meetings, automation  
- Creative 🎨: Ideation, poetry, visual and narrative prompts  
- Prompt Engineering ⚙️: Meta-prompts, QA/testing, voice and style control  

---

## 🔍 Discovery & Maintenance

To keep prompts effective and relevant, the library embraces best practices such as:

- Clear, unambiguous instructions  
- Completeness and effectiveness in outcome  
- Reusability across related use cases  
- Regular reviews and quality assessment  
- Lifecycle management: Draft → Active → Archived

---

## 🚀 How to Use This Library

1. Create new prompts using the provided template  
2. Assign appropriate categories, intent, and attributes  
3. Write clear instructions and add examples  
4. Review and test the prompt for quality  
5. Promote to active when ready for use  
6. Continuously update and improve based on feedback
