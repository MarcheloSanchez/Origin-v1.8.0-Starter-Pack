# 🚿 Bathroom Tasks

> [!tip]+ **Quick Reference**
> **Why**: Bathroom is health-critical (bacteria, moisture control). Quick cleanup daily prevents mold & odors
> **When**: Daily basics always, weekly deep on Tuesday, monthly deep once/month
> **Time**: Daily (2-3 min) + Weekly (8 min) + Monthly (15-20 min)

---

## ✅ DAILY ROUTINE (2-3 min)

**After shower / As needed:**
- [ ] Wipe mirror
- [ ] Quick toilet brush if needed (quick swish & flush)
- [ ] Wipe sink splashes
- [ ] Hang towels straight
- [ ] Put items back in place

**Why daily**: Moisture control prevents mold. Quick wipe prevents buildup.

---

## 📅 WEEKLY ROUTINE - Tuesday (8 min)

**"Deep Clean Tuesday" for bathroom:**
- [ ] Scrub toilet (bowl + exterior base)
- [ ] Scrub shower/tub walls (top to bottom, corners)
- [ ] Wipe shower door/curtain rod
- [ ] Mop floor thoroughly
- [ ] Wipe all mirrors (streak-free)
- [ ] Restock supplies (soap, toilet paper, etc.)

**Timing**: 8 minutes if daily is maintained

**Why important**: Prevents soap scum, mold, and bacteria growth. Moisture-prone areas need weekly attention.

---

## 🔍 MONTHLY DEEP-CLEAN (15-20 min) - Bathroom Focus Month

**Do this ONCE per month when it's "Bathroom Week"**

### Tile & Grout (10 min)
- [ ] Scrub shower/tub tile grout with brush
- [ ] Clean tile corners where mold hides
- [ ] Wipe down shower walls completely
- [ ] Check caulking for mold (if found, note for repair)

### Fixtures & Hardware (5 min)
- [ ] Scrub faucet & handles (limescale removal)
- [ ] Clean toilet base & around pipes
- [ ] Wipe light fixtures & exhaust fan cover
- [ ] Clean baseboards (often hidden dust)

### Organization & Refresh (3 min)
- [ ] Wipe down shower caddy/shelves
- [ ] Throw out dried-up bottles/expired products
- [ ] Reorganize cabinets
- [ ] Check for leaks or dripping faucets

### Ventilation Check (2 min)
- [ ] Exhaust fan: Run it during shower to check operation
- [ ] If fan is blocked/dusty: Note for maintenance

---

## 🎯 Bathroom Success Standards

**Daily-Check Level**: "Usable and fresh-smelling"
- Mirror clear
- Sink clean
- Toilet flushed & brush clean
- No wet puddles on floor
- No odors

**Weekly Level**: "Sparkling clean"
- Toilet scrubbed (bowl white)
- Shower/tub wall clean
- Floor mopped
- All surfaces shine
- Supplies stocked

**Monthly Level**: "Professional clean"
- Grout spotless
- No soap scum anywhere
- Exhaust fan working
- Under sink organized
- Towel bars/fixtures clean

---

## 💡 Pro Tips for Efficiency

| Challenge | Solution |
|-----------|----------|
| "Mold/mildew growing" | Run exhaust fan during + 30 min after shower. Wipe steam weekly. |
| "Soap scum buildup" | Squeegee shower after use. Weekly scrub prevents accumulation. |
| "Hard water stains" | White vinegar spray on faucets; wipe after shower |
| "Grout looks dark" | Weekly brush scrub prevents heavy staining; mold preventive |
| "Slow drains" | Monthly: pour baking soda + vinegar down drain, let sit |
| "Toilet ring stains" | Toilet bowl cleaner + brush; let sit 10 min for tough stains |
| "Mirror streaks" | Microfiber cloth + water/vinegar. One direction only |

## ⚠️ Warning Signs for Maintenance

Keep eye out for:
- [ ] Black mold in corners/grout
- [ ] Slow drain (possible clog)
- [ ] Dripping faucet (water waste)
- [ ] Exhaust fan not working (moisture trap)
- [ ] Caulk deteriorating (water infiltration risk)

*Note maintenance issues in monthly review*

---

## 🔄 Routine Variations

**If super busy this week?**
- Daily only (mirror, sink wipe)
- Skip weekly, do next week

**If have extra time?**
- Deep clean the grout
- Organize under-sink cabinet
- Clean inside towel bar

**Quarterly Maintenance (Professional Level)?**
- Grout sealing (prevents staining)
- Caulk replacement if needed
- Deep exhaust fan cleaning (remove cover, blow out dust)
