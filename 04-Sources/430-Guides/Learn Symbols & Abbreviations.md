## 🧱 Fundamental Note Types
There are only a few **atomic units** of useful notes. Master these, and you'll be unstoppable:
1. **Facts** (e.g. "The mitochondria is the powerhouse of the cell")
2. **Relationships** (e.g. "X causes Y", "A is part of B", "If A, then B")
3. **Questions** (e.g. "Why does X cause Y?", "What is A made of?")
4. **Insights** (your own takeaways, e.g. "Oh! A is like B")
5. **Mental Models** (reusable frameworks)
6. **Visual Maps** (sketches, graphs, diagrams)
Every good note is one or more of these — compressed into shorthand or visual form.
## ✍️ How to Write Fast, Easy, and Readable Notes
### 1. **Use Personal Abbreviations and Symbols**
Don’t worry about “standard” notation. Create **your own system of symbols and abbreviations**, and make sure it’s instantly decodable to **you**.
Examples:
- → for cause/effect
- ∵ for "because"
- ↑↓ for increase/decrease
- ? for something you don’t understand
- ⊂ for “is a part of”
- ⚠️ for critical point
- 💡 for idea or insight
- 💭 for question
- - for definition
- [⏱️] for time-sensitive process
- ❗for "remember this"
Make your own and make them **unambiguous** and **consistent**. They will become _your own language_ for thinking.
