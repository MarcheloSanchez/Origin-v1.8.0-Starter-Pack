## 🎮 Daily Routine for Max XP

**Morning (6-9am):**
1. Create daily note (+5 XP × 1.2 = 6 XP)
2. Process inbox to zero (+25 XP × 1.2 = 30 XP)
3. Check daily challenge (+50-75 XP)

**Midday (9-12pm):**
4. Work on projects (task XP)
5. Create/promote notes (5-50 XP each)

**Afternoon (12-5pm):**
6. Continue project work
7. Add links to notes (+1 XP each)

**Evening (5-8pm):**
8. Weekly review if Friday (+25 XP)
9. Update MOCs (+30 XP)

**Night (8-10pm):**
10. Daily note reflection
11. Tomorrow planning

**Total Potential:** 150-300+ XP per day

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- ❌ Creating low-quality notes for XP
- ❌ Ignoring streaks (they compound!)
- ❌ Skipping weekly reviews
- ❌ Forgetting multipliers
- ❌ All-or-nothing thinking

**Instead:**
- ✅ Focus on sustainable quality
- ✅ Maintain daily note streak
- ✅ Never skip reviews
- ✅ Use morning/weekend bonuses
- ✅ Progress over perfection
