## 💡Prompt [Bring your dreams to life]

## 📝Description 
1. List your future goals and desires: Open a doc and write down everything you want to have, do, and be in the future. Think BIG! The wilder, the better.  
  
2. Bring your vision to life with ChatGPT: Type, “Tell me a story of a day in the life as my future self based on this description.” Copy and paste your goals and let ChatGPT paint the picture!  
  
3. Immerse yourself in the vision: Read through the story of “future you.” Feel it, visualize it, and let it really sink in. This is where you’re heading.  
  
4. Create an action plan: Ask ChatGPT to “create a daily schedule that will help me achieve this life.” It’ll give you a step-by-step plan, complete with time-blocking and action steps, to make your vision a reality.
