# 🍳 Kitchen Tasks

> [!tip]+ **Quick Reference**
> **Why**: Kitchen is high-traffic, health-critical (food prep), and impacts daily mood
> **When**: Daily basics always, weekly deep on Monday, monthly deep once/month
> **Time**: Daily (3 min) + Weekly (8 min) + Monthly (20-30 min)

---

## ✅ DAILY ROUTINE (3-5 min)

**After meals / Before bed:**
- [ ] Wash dishes or load dishwasher
- [ ] Wipe down counters (any crumbs/spills)
- [ ] Put items back in place
- [ ] Sweep floor if needed
- [ ] Sink sparkling (quick rinse)

**Why daily**: Prevents buildup that feels overwhelming. Clean kitchen = better cooking mood.

---

## 📅 WEEKLY ROUTINE - Monday (8 min)

**"Clean & Inspect" Monday:**
- [ ] Wipe all cabinet fronts & handles
- [ ] Clean microwave inside (30 sec steam+wipe)
- [ ] Wipe refrigerator exterior & door handles
- [ ] Wipe stovetop thoroughly (top + knobs)
- [ ] Mop floor (quick sweep then mop)
- [ ] Clean window/glass if applicable

**Timing**: 8 minutes if daily is maintained

---

## 🔍 MONTHLY DEEP-CLEAN (20-30 min) - Kitchen Focus Month

**Do this ONCE per month when it's "Kitchen Week"**

### Inside-Outs (15 min)
- [ ] Fridge: Pull out drawers, wipe shelves, toss expired items
- [ ] Wipe inside microwave (walls, turntable)
- [ ] Clean oven exterior & glass
- [ ] Wipe down all cabinet fronts/tops
- [ ] Clean light fixtures/under cabinets

### Appliance Refresh (10 min)
- [ ] Stove: Remove grates, wipe underneath, clean knobs
- [ ] Fridge coils (once per month): Vacuum/brush back of fridge
- [ ] Dishwasher filter: Check & rinse if needed
- [ ] Sink drain: Hot water + baking soda rinse

### Flooring (5 min)
- [ ] Sweep thoroughly (including under appliances/tables)
- [ ] Mop with appropriate cleaner
- [ ] Check baseboards, wipe if dusty

---

## 🎯 Kitchen Success Standards

**Daily-Check Level**: "Could someone drop by without embarrassment?"
- Counters clear & clean
- No dishes in sink
- Floor clear of debris
- No visible spills

**Weekly Level**: "Kitchen is fully functional and clean"
- Appliances wiped down
- Floor mopped
- Handles/switches clean
- Everything organized

**Monthly Level**: "Kitchen is inspection-ready"
- Deep clean visible
- Organized drawers/cabinets
- Refrigerator organized
- No sticky spots anywhere

---

## 💡 Pro Tips for Efficiency

| Challenge | Solution |
|-----------|----------|
| "Dishes pile up" | Wash immediately or load dishwasher right after eating |
| "Sticky floors" | Wipe spills immediately; mop only takes 3 min if done right |
| "Bacteria growing" | Kitchen cloth in bleach water once/week; sponge replaced monthly |
| "Grease buildup" | Wipe stovetop after cooking while warm (much easier) |
| "Organized clutter" | Assign homes for items; daily reset takes 2 min |
| "Fridge smells" | Open box of baking soda; replace every 3 months |

## 🔄 Routine Variations

**If super busy this week?**
- Daily only (dishes + wipe counters)
- Skip weekly, do next week

**If have extra time?**
- Add fridge wipe
- Organize one cabinet
- Clean inside microwave

**Quarterly (when it's Kitchen Month in deep-clean rotation)?**
- Do the full monthly deep-clean above
- Plus: cabinets inventory, expired food audit, organize drawers
