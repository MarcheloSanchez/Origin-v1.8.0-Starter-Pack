## 🔄 Calendar System Workflows

> - What's my primary focus for today?
> - Which Area needs attention?
> - What energy level do I have?

> - What's one insight to remember?
> - How did my Areas/Efforts progress?

> - Which Effort(s) require significant progress?
> - What schedule adjustments would optimize energy/effectiveness?
> - What insights from this week should inform next week's approach?
> - What experiments or improvements to try?

# About Calendar Templates

| **Monthly** | Strategic assessment | Areas health check | Every month |
| **Quarterly** | OKRs and major initiatives | Strategic area evaluation | Every quarter |
| **Yearly** | Vision and major themes | Complete system review | Every year |
