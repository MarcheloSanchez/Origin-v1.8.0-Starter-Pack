# 🏠 Home Maintenance System

> [!info]+ **Quick Reference**
> **Objective**: Maintain clean, functional home with minimal mental overhead
> **Scope**: 4-6 rooms, divided into daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly tasks
> **Success**: Consistent completion without overwhelm, visible progress
> **Linked Areas**: Health (clean environment), Personal (home pride), Finance (prevent costly repairs)
> **Duration**: Ongoing system (review quarterly)

---

## 🎯 Objective - Goal

**What**: Create a sustainable home maintenance routine organized by room, trackable but not overwhelming

**Why**:
- Clean, organized home reduces stress and improves daily wellbeing
- Preventive maintenance saves 5-10x in repair costs
- Visual progress on tangible tasks builds momentum for other efforts
- Health benefit: Clean spaces improve sleep, energy, mental clarity

**Success looks like**:
- Weekly baseline tasks completed 80%+ of time
- No "crisis cleaning" required
- Monthly deep-clean zones rotating (one room/week gets focus)
- Quarterly major maintenance prevented through proactive tracking

---

## 📋 Scope & Requirements

### Must Have (Non-Negotiable)
- **Daily essentials** (5-10 min): Kitchen clean-up, bathroom basics, bedroom tidying
- **Weekly targets** (30-40 min total): Focused cleaning by room, rotation prevents overwhelm
- **Monthly deep-clean** (1 room per week = manageable cadence)
- **Quarterly reviews**: HVAC, filters, seasonal tasks

### Nice to Have (Flexibility)
- Seasonal decorating/reorganization
- Advanced organization systems
- Guest-ready deep cleans
- Specialized projects (painting, landscaping)

---

## 🏗️ System Architecture

### Room Divisions (Adjust to Your Home)

1. **🍳 Kitchen** - High-touch daily area
2. **🚿 Bathroom** - Health-critical, moisture control
3. **🛏️ Bedroom** - Sleep quality zone
4. **🛋️ Living Areas** - Social/work space
5. **🚗 Entry/Laundry** - Transition zones
6. **📚 Office/Study** - Focus space (if applicable)

*Each room gets its own tracking file with task breakdown*

### Task Frequency Pyramid

```
DAILY (5-10 min)
└─ Kitchen: dishes, wipe counters, sweep
   Bathroom: mirror, sink, toilet
   Bedroom: make bed, clear clutter

WEEKLY (40 min total, ~7 min per room)
└─ Kitchen: deep clean fridge, wipe appliances
   Bathroom: clean shower/tub, mop floor
   Bedroom: change sheets, vacuum
   Living: dust, vacuum, mirrors
   Entry: sweep, organize shoes

MONTHLY (4 zones = 1 zone per week)
└─ Deep clean ONE room thoroughly
   Week 1: Kitchen (baseboards, inside cabinets)
   Week 2: Bathroom (grout, organize)
   Week 3: Bedroom (under bed, closet refresh)
   Week 4: Living (furniture move, deep dust)

QUARTERLY (4 zones = seasonal)
└─ HVAC filter change
   Deep organization one room
   Seasonal maintenance (gutters, AC/heat prep)
```

## 💪 Getting Started (Not Overwhelming!)

### Phase 1: Establish Baseline (Week 1)
- [ ] Choose 1-2 rooms to start with (kitchen + bathroom recommended)
- [ ] Do ONE daily routine pass - just track time
- [ ] Read your chosen [[🏠 Room Tasks]] file
- [ ] Estimate if tasks fit in suggested timeframe

### Phase 2: Build Habit (Weeks 2-4)
- [ ] Daily routine 5-6 days per week
- [ ] Complete first week's room tasks
- [ ] Adjust time estimates based on reality
- [ ] If going well, add second room

### Phase 3: Add Rotation (Weeks 5-8)
- [ ] Daily routine 6-7 days per week (mostly consistent)
- [ ] Weekly room rotation established
- [ ] One monthly deep-clean scheduled
- [ ] Track completion percentage

### Phase 4: Optimize (Month 2+)
- [ ] Full system running (all rooms in rotation)
- [ ] Monthly deep-cleans on track (1 room per week)
- [ ] Quarterly seasonal tasks scheduled
- [ ] Adjust based on what's realistic vs aspirational

## 🎯 Success Metrics

- **Consistency**: Aiming for 80%+ weekly task completion (not perfectionism)
- **Time tracking**: Actual vs estimated - adjust if needed
- **Completion rate**: By room and frequency
- **Satisfaction**: Monthly reflection on "does home feel good?"

---

## 🚧 Potential Blockers & Solutions

| Blocker | Mitigation |
|---------|-----------|
| "Too overwhelming, too many tasks" | Start with ONLY daily routine first. Add one weekly room. |
| "Can't find time for 40 min/week" | Compress to 20 min by doing 2-3 min per room. |
| "No motivation to start" | Do ONE 10-minute "quick win" today (one room surface clean). |
| "Inconsistent - missing days" | Move to specific day/time (e.g., "Every Sunday 10am"). |
| "Tasks take longer than estimated" | Adjust estimates - okay to take 15 min vs 8 min. |
| "Perfectionism trap" | Remember: 80% consistency beats 100% one week then nothing. |
