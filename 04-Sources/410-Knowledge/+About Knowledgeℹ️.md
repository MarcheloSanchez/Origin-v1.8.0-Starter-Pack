## 🩺 Knowledge Development Health Diagnostics

### **🔴 Red Flags - Address Learning Gaps**

> [!danger]+ **Learning System Problems**
> - ❌ **Knowledge Decay** - Completed courses with no practical application or skill retention
> - ❌ **Shiny Object Syndrome** - Starting multiple courses without completing any
> - ❌ **Credential Collecting** - Pursuing certifications without career relevance or practical value
> - ❌ **Isolation Learning** - Educational experiences not connected to career goals or current projects
> - ❌ **Passive Consumption** - Completing courses without hands-on practice or real-world application

### **🟡 Yellow Warnings - Optimize Learning Strategy**

> [!warning]+ **Learning Effectiveness Opportunities**
> - ⚠️ **Surface Processing** - Completing content without deep understanding or synthesis
> - ⚠️ **Application Gap** - Learning valuable skills but not implementing in work or personal projects
> - ⚠️ **Knowledge Silos** - Educational experiences not connecting to broader expertise development
> - ⚠️ **Outdated Skills** - Learning in areas becoming less relevant to career direction
> - ⚠️ **Solo Learning** - Missing opportunities for collaborative learning and peer discussion

### **🟢 Green Health Indicators**

> [!success]+ **Strategic Knowledge Development**
> - ✅ **Applied Learning** - Immediately implementing new skills in real projects and situations
> - ✅ **Career Alignment** - Educational choices directly supporting professional goals and Areas
> - ✅ **Teaching Others** - Sharing knowledge through mentoring, workshops, or documentation
> - ✅ **Continuous Practice** - Regular skill maintenance and advancement beyond initial learning
> - ✅ **Knowledge Integration** - Connecting new learning to existing expertise and broader understanding

## 🎯 What “Knowledge” Means Here
Knowledge is the **network of ideas** you actively use—not a pile of notes. Practically, it lives as:
- **Dots** → individual knowledge nodes (Atomics, Areas, People, Places, Tools)  
- **MOCs** → curated navigation maps that organize clusters and storylines  
- **Sources** → external inputs that feed and refine your own ideas

---

## 🧩 Sub-types & Where They Live
- **Atomics (💡100)**: one idea per note; building blocks for everything else  
- **MOCs (🗺️01)**: index/guide notes that curate relationships and pathways  
- **Source Notes (📚04)**: processed references that *explain why they matter*  
- **Efforts (🚀03)**: where knowledge is **applied** and refined by doing

> [!example]+ **From Source → Atomic → MOC → Effort**
> Read (Source) → extract insights (Atomic) → place in topic map (MOC) → use in a project (Effort)

---

## 🌱 Knowledge Lifecycle
**📤 Seed** → **🌱 Seedling** → **🪴 Sapling** → **🌲 Evergreen** → **🍓 Fruit (applied insight)**  
- **Seed**: quick capture with minimal context  
- **Seedling**: short explanation + 1–2 links  
- **Sapling**: examples/evidence + multiple links  
- **Evergreen**: stable, reusable; appears in a MOC  
- **Fruit**: drives a decision, habit, or deliverable

> Tip: Promotion triggers—**add to MOC** when an idea is referenced repeatedly or guides other notes.

---

## 🩺 Knowledge Health Diagnostics
**🔴 Critical**
- Orphan atomics (0 links)  
- MOCs that read like raw dumps (no curation)  
- Sources kept as clippings without interpretation

**🟡 Needs Attention**
- Notes link out but don’t explain *how* they relate  
- Many duplicates competing for the same concept  
- Maturity stages not progressing for months

**🟢 Healthy**
- 3–5 meaningful links per atomic  
- MOCs contain short scope paragraph + curated “includes”  
- Regular review cycles; insights show up in Efforts

---

## 🔄 Working Loops
- **Daily (5–10m)**: capture 1–3 seeds; add at least one link per processed note  
- **Weekly (20–30m)**: connect new atomics; prune duplicates; update 1 MOC  
- **Monthly (45–60m)**: promote key topics, collapse/merge overlaps, snapshot wins  
- **Quarterly (60–90m)**: architecture review—rename, split/merge, re-index big topics

## 🔗 Integration Network
- **Dots** — your node graph for living knowledge  
- **MOCs** — curated maps for navigation and teaching  
- **Sources** — pipelines from the outside world  
- **Efforts** — application arena; export patterns back into knowledge

**Dashboards/Queries** surface: new seeds, orphan notes, stale MOCs, and recently promoted evergreens.

---

## ✅ Definition of Done (DoD) — Knowledge Items
- **Atomic**: clear one-liner summary; ≥1 meaningful link; tagged/type set; status ≠ draft  
- **MOC**: scope paragraph; hand-picked links; one query/table/diagram; up-to-date  
- **Source**: why it matters; extracted insights; links to related atomics/MOCs

---

## 🚀 Getting Started
1. Convert one recent highlight into an **Atomic** (one idea, one file).  
2. Add **two links**: one to a related atomic, one to a MOC.  
3. Update one **MOC** with a short scope note and 5 curated links.  
4. Apply one evergreen idea in an **Effort**, and record the result as “🍓 Fruit”.
