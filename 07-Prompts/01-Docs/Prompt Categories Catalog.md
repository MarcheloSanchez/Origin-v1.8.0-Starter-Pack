# 🗂 Prompt Categories – Canonical Value List

**Purpose:**  
Single reference for all allowed category and subcategory slugs.  
Use these slugs in the YAML fields `prompt_category` and `prompt_subcategory`.  
Each prompt should use **exactly one category** and **1–3 subcategories** that logically belong.

---

## ✅ Allowed Categories (with short definitions)

`[writing, coding, business, education, productivity, creative, prompt-engineering]`

|Category|Description|
|---|---|
|**writing**|Plan, compose, or refine text for human readers.|
|**coding**|Generate, review, or explain code and dev artifacts.|
|**business**|Strategy, product, GTM, ops, and reporting.|
|**education**|Teaching, learning, and assessment prompts.|
|**productivity**|Workflows, PKM systems, and decision support.|
|**creative**|Artistic, storytelling, or visual ideation tasks.|
|**prompt-engineering**|Meta-prompts and prompt-library operations.|

## 📝 Category Trees with Definitions

### 📝 Writing

_Def:_ Plan and craft human-readable text.

- blog-posts – outlines, drafts, SEO structures
    
- emails-outreach – cold emails, follow-ups, replies
    
- storytelling – narrative, hooks, arcs, characters
    
- technical-docs – specs, READMEs, API docs
    
- copywriting-ads – headlines, landing pages, ads
    
- summaries-paraphrase – condense or simplify text
    

---

### 💻 Coding

_Def:_ Produce, analyze, or document software and algorithms.

- code-gen – generate code from requirements
    
- code-review – critique code clarity and quality
    
- debugging – locate and fix errors
    
- refactoring – improve structure without changing behavior
    
- docs-comments – documentation and inline comments
    
- data-algorithms – data structures and algorithms practice
    

---

### 🏢 Business

_Def:_ Strategy, product, and organizational operations.

- strategy-planning – goals, roadmaps, prioritization
    
- product-management – PRDs, user stories, acceptance criteria
    
- marketing – ICP, messaging, channels
    
- sales-outreach – prospecting, demos, scripts
    
- ops-sops – checklists, process docs, SOPs
    
- analytics-reporting – KPIs, dashboards, insight writing
    
- legal-policy – policies, memos, compliance
    

---

### 🎓 Education

_Def:_ Teaching, explaining, and evaluating learning.

- lesson-design – instructional structure and goals
    
- assessment – quizzes, rubrics, exam items
    
- tutoring-explain – ELI5 → expert explanations
    
- study-guides – outlines, flashcards, summaries
    
- qa-banks – item banks by topic and difficulty
    
- feedback-rubrics – grading criteria and exemplars
    

---

### 📈 Productivity

_Def:_ Workflows, systems, and decision processes.

- tasking-checklists – action lists, SOPs, recurring workflows
    
- decision-support – tradeoffs, matrices, risk notes
    
- meetings – agendas, summaries, follow-ups
    
- research-synthesis – gather and summarize insights
    
- pkm-notes – note patterns, Zettelkasten, MOCs
    
- automation – scripts, templates, integrations
    
- personal-growth – habits, journaling, coaching
    
- career-materials – CVs, portfolios, interview prep
    

---

### 🎨 Creative

_Def:_ Art, story, and design ideation.

- ideation-brainstorms – divergent idea generation
    
- poetry-prose – poems, microfiction, prose
    
- visual-prompts – text for image generation
    
- game-narrative-design – quests, pacing, systems
    
- worldbuilding-characters – settings, lore, characters
    
- scripts-dialogue – stage/screen dialogue
    

---

### 🧩 Prompt Engineering (Meta)

_Def:_ Prompts about prompting and library management.

- meta-prompts-templates – reusable prompt shells
    
- prompt-qa-testing – evaluation and testing of prompts
    
- style-voice-roleplay – persona, tone, voice control
    
- library-ops – naming, tagging, migrations
    
- safety-constraints – guardrails, policy compliance
