## Categories & Subcategories (with purposes)

### Writing — produce and polish text
- **Blog Posts** — Long-form articles and outlines  
- **Emails** — Outreach, follow-ups, support replies  
- **Storytelling** — Plot, character, scenes, beats  
- **Technical Docs** — Specs, READMEs, user guides  
- **Copywriting/Ads** — Headlines, CTAs, landing pages  
- **Summaries/Paraphrase** — Condense or restyle text

### Coding — generate, improve, and reason about code
- **Code Gen** — Scaffolds, functions, tests
- **Code Review** — Comments, readability, smells
- **Debugging** — Hypotheses, repro steps, fixes
- **Refactoring** — Structure, patterns, performance
- **Docs & Comments** — API docs, examples, inline notes
- **Data/Algorithms** — Complexity, examples, correctness

### Business — planning, growth, and operations
- **Strategy & Planning** — Roadmaps, SWOT, OKRs
- **Marketing** — ICP, messaging, content calendars
- **Sales & Outreach** — Scripts, sequences, objections
- **Ops & SOPs** — Checklists, playbooks, handoffs
- **Analytics & Reporting** — Metrics, dashboards, insights
- **Legal & Policy** — Plain-English summaries and checklists

### Education — teach, learn, and assess
- **Lesson Design** — Objectives, activities, scaffolds
- **Assessment** — Question banks, rubrics, grading notes
- **Tutoring/Explain** — Step-by-step, analogies, misconceptions
- **Study Guides** — Spaced repetition, cheat sheets
- **Q&A Banks** — Leveled practice questions by topic
- **Feedback & Rubrics** — Criterion-based improvement guidance

### Productivity — organize work and decisions
- **Tasking & Checklists** — Templates, SOP inserts
- **Decision Support** — Pros/cons, weighted scoring
- **Meetings** — Agendas, notes, action extraction
- **Research & Synthesis** — Source triage, briefs
- **PKM/Notes** — Capture, link, summarize, refine
- **Automation** — Tool-calling, workflow prompts

### Creative — invent concepts and media
- **Ideation/Brainstorms** — Lists, lateral thinking
- **Poetry & Prose** — Styles, constraints, voices
- **Visual Prompts** — Art styles, render specs
- **Game/Narrative Design** — Mechanics, quests, arcs
- **Worldbuilding/Characters** — Settings, bibles, profiles
- **Scripts/Dialogue** — Scenes, beats, revisions
