Solicit constructive feedback regarding the clarity, effectiveness, and engagement of the presented content. Encourage specific insights on strengths and weaknesses, suggesting tangible improvements. Include questions that guide respondents to share what resonated most, what seemed unclear or lacking, and how they suggest addressing any identified gaps.
