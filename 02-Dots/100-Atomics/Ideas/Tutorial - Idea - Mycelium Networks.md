# Mycelium Networks

> [!orbit] Tutorial Example
> This note demonstrates maturity evolution from 📤seed → 🌱seedling → 🪴sapling

---

## One-liner

Trees communicate, share resources, and support each other through underground mycelium networks - demonstrating that robust systems emerge from **connections**, not isolation.

---

## The Insight

Individual trees appear separate above ground, but below the surface they're connected through vast fungal networks. This "Wood Wide Web" enables:

1. **Resource Sharing**: Older trees send nutrients to younger, shaded seedlings
2. **Information Transfer**: Warning signals about pests travel faster than chemical signals through air
3. **Network Resilience**: Multiple pathways mean damage to one tree doesn't collapse the system
4. **Emergent Intelligence**: The network "decides" where resources are most needed

---

## Why This Matters

The mycelium network is a perfect metaphor for knowledge management:

| Forest Network | PKM System |
|----------------|------------|
| Individual trees | Individual notes |
| Mycelium connections | Wikilinks and backlinks |
| Nutrient sharing | Idea cross-pollination |
| Mother trees | MOCs and hub notes |
| Warning signals | Pattern recognition |
| Ecosystem health | Knowledge coherence |

> "A forest is not a collection of trees. It's a connected system that thinks together."
