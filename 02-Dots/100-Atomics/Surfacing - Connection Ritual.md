# Connection Ritual

A small daily ritual (link every new note to 2–3 existing notes) increases cross-pollination and creates the edges that algorithms and dashboards use to surface material.

Practical: During processing, add a "Connections" section and create at least two backlinks to related atomics or MOCs.

Metric: count of inlinks per note over 30 days.
