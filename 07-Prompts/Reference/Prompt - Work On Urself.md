## 💡Prompt [note]

## 📝Description 
1. Personal Trainer on Demand:  
“Create a 4-week workout plan for fat loss and muscle building I can do at home with no equipment.”  
Make it progressive so I can see results week by week.  
  
2. Meal Plan Generator:  
“Give me a high-protein, low-calorie meal plan (around 2000 calories) to help me lose weight while keeping muscle.”  
Include simple meals I can prep fast or grab on the go.  
  
3. Daily Habit Tracker:  
“Build me a daily tracker for workouts, water intake, steps, and sleep.”  
Make it easy to follow so I can stay consistent without overthinking it.  
  
4. Snack Finder:  
“List 10 high-protein snacks under 200 calories I can buy or prep easily.”  
Help me crush cravings and hit my protein goals.  
  
5. Motivation + Accountability:  
“Send me a motivational quote and accountability reminder daily to stay focused on my fitness goals.”  
Make it personal so it actually hits.
