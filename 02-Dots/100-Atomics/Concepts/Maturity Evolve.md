# Maturity Tracking 🌱

Track the growth and development of your ideas, notes, or projects through these maturity stages:

| Stage     | Icon | Description                        | Attributes                    | Next Steps                         |
| --------- | ---- | ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| seed      | 📤   | Initial capture, raw idea or note  | Minimal content, no links     | Clarify intent, add metadata       |
| seedling  | 🌱   | Early development                  | Basic content, some links     | Expand, add context, connect notes |
| sapling   | 🪴   | Growing, more structure and detail | Rich content, multiple links  | Develop evidence, cross-link more  |
| evergreen | 🌲   | Mature, stable knowledge asset     | Well-structured, reused often | Maintain, apply, share             |
| fruit     | 🍓   | Valuable, actionable insight       | Evidenced, widely applicable  | Leverage, teach, integrate further |

---

## Maturity Lifecycle
📤 → 🌱 → 🪴 → 🌲 →  🍓

## How to Use

- **Update maturity tag** as your note evolves.
- **Review regularly** to advance stages or archive.
- **Focus on progression:** From Seed 📤 to Fruit 🍓.
