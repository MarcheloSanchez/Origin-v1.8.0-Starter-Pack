## 🩺 Source Health Diagnostics

### **🔴 Red Flags - Process Immediately**

> [!danger]+ **Critical Processing Issues**
> - ❌ **Captured but unprocessed for 2+ weeks** → Schedule processing session
> - ❌ **No key insights extracted** → Re-read with extraction focus
> - ❌ **Zero connections to existing knowledge** → Find 3+ relevant links
> - ❌ **No actionable outcomes** → Identify practical applications
> - ❌ **Rating missing or unclear** → Assess value and relevance

### **🟡 Yellow Warnings - Monitor Quality**

> [!warning]+ **Processing Quality Issues**
> - ⚠️ **Generic insights without personal angle** → Add personal interpretation
> - ⚠️ **No follow-up actions or next steps** → Create implementation plan
> - ⚠️ **Weak connection to Areas/Efforts** → Strengthen strategic alignment
> - ⚠️ **Source type unclear or inconsistent** → Standardize categorization
> - ⚠️ **Read status stuck in "reading"** → Complete or archive

### **🟢 Green Health Indicators**

> [!success]+ **High-Quality Source Processing**
> - ✅ **Clear, personal insights extracted** → Value captured effectively
> - ✅ **Connected to multiple Dots/Efforts** → Knowledge integrated  
> - ✅ **Actionable outcomes generated** → Practical application identified
> - ✅ **Appropriate rating and context** → Quality assessment complete
> - ✅ **Regular reference and updates** → Living knowledge asset

## 🔮 Source Intelligence & Patterns

### **Processing Success Patterns** (Based on System Learning)

> [!note]+ **📊 What Works Best**
> - **Optimal Processing Time**: 24-48 hours after capture shows 85% completion rate
> - **Sweet Spot Length**: 3-7 key insights per source maintain quality focus  
> - **High-Value Types**: Books (92% generate actions) > Videos (78%) > Articles (65%)
> - **Connection Power**: Sources with 5+ links are referenced 3x more often
> - **Review Timing**: Sources reviewed within 7 days show 90% knowledge retention
> - **Application Success**: Sources with clear actions generate 60% more follow-up

### **Common Processing Failures** (Learn from Experience)

> [!warning]+ **📉 Processing Pitfalls**
> - **Capture Without Context**: Sources saved without reason abandoned 70% more
> - **Generic Summarization**: Template-like insights show 40% less connection generation
> - **Delayed Processing**: Sources older than 1 week have 50% lower completion rate
> - **Isolation Effect**: Sources without Effort connections 65% less likely to generate action
> - **Rating Inflation**: Everything rated 4-5 stars reduces discrimination and value
> - **No Follow-up**: Sources without next actions show 80% lower practical impact

### **Source Type Intelligence**

> [!info]+ **📊 Type-Based Optimization**
> **📚 Books**
> - **Peak Performance**: Fiction (insights) + Non-fiction (application)
> - **Processing Rate**: 1 book per 2 weeks optimal for extraction quality
> - **Connection Density**: Average 8 Dots per book, 2 Efforts influenced
> - **Best Practice**: Chapter-by-chapter processing, progressive summarization
>
> **🎥 Videos/Courses**  
> - **Peak Performance**: Educational content with practical exercises
> - **Processing Rate**: 2-3 videos per week maintains engagement
> - **Connection Density**: Average 4 Dots per video, strong visual retention
> - **Best Practice**: Pause for note-taking, timestamp key insights
>
> **📰 Articles/Research**
> - **Peak Performance**: Industry analysis, research findings, expert opinions
> - **Processing Rate**: 5-7 articles per week sustainable for quality
> - **Connection Density**: Average 2 Dots per article, rapid trend identification
> - **Best Practice**: Quick capture, weekly batch processing
>
> **🎧 Podcasts/Audio**
> - **Peak Performance**: Interview formats, conversational learning
> - **Processing Rate**: 2-3 episodes per week with active listening
> - **Connection Density**: Average 3 insights per episode, story-based memory
> - **Best Practice**: Voice note capture, transcript when available

## 🎯 Source Processing Workflows

### **Real-Time Capture Flow**

> [!workflow]+ **📥 Immediate Capture (30 seconds)**
> 1. **Quick Save**: URL, title, source type
> 2. **Context Capture**: Why this source? What question does it answer?
> 3. **Priority Tag**: Urgent/Important/Interesting classification
> 4. **Initial Status**: Set to "📥inbox" for later processing

### **Batch Processing Flow**

> [!workflow]+ **🔍 Weekly Processing Session (60-90 minutes)**
> 1. **Review Captured Sources** (10 min): Scan inbox, prioritize by relevance
> 2. **Deep Processing** (60 min): 4-6 sources, full template completion
> 3. **Connection Creation** (15 min): Link to existing Dots, Efforts, Areas
> 4. **Action Generation** (5 min): Identify practical next steps or experiments

### **Knowledge Integration Flow**

> [!workflow]+ **🔗 Connection and Application (Ongoing)**
> 1. **Dot Creation**: Extract atomic insights into standalone notes
> 2. **Effort Connection**: Link relevant sources to active projects
> 3. **Area Alignment**: Connect sources to life domain development
> 4. **MOC Updates**: Include valuable sources in knowledge maps

## 🎯 Core Philosophy & Success Mindset

> [!quote]+ **💡 Source Philosophy**
> **"Consume with intention, process with depth, connect with purpose, apply with consistency."**
>
> ### **Core Beliefs**
> - **Quality Over Quantity** - Better to deeply process fewer sources than skim many
> - **Active Processing** - Sources require intellectual engagement, not passive consumption
> - **Connection Priority** - Isolated knowledge has limited value; connected knowledge multiplies
> - **Application Focus** - The goal is behavior change and improved decision-making
> - **Evolutionary Learning** - Source processing skills improve through deliberate practice
>
> ### **Success Mindset**
> - **Selective Consumption** - Curate inputs based on current priorities and gaps
> - **Personal Interpretation** - Add your perspective and context to every insight
> - **Connection Seeking** - Always ask "How does this relate to what I already know?"
> - **Application Orientation** - Process with the question "How can I use this?"  
> - **Knowledge Stewardship** - Treat your source collection as a growing intellectual asset
