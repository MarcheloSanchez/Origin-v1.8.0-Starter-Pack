## TL;DR — 5 Bullets

1. **Systems are interconnected elements** organized coherently to achieve something - the relationships matter more than the parts
2. **Feedback loops** create self-reinforcing or balancing behavior - small changes can cascade into large effects
3. **Emergence** means the whole is greater than the sum of parts - system behavior can't be predicted from elements alone
4. **Leverage points** are places where small interventions create large change - finding them is the art of systems thinking
5. **Counterintuitive results** are common - systems often behave opposite to what we expect

---

## Key Insights

### 1. "A system is more than the sum of its parts"
Emergence happens when elements interact. You can't understand a forest by studying individual trees.

### 2. Feedback Loops Drive Behavior
- **Reinforcing loops**: Success breeds success (compound growth)
- **Balancing loops**: Self-correction toward equilibrium
- Most interesting behavior comes from interaction of multiple loops

### 3. Delays Cause Oscillation
When feedback is delayed, systems overshoot and undershoot. This explains boom/bust cycles.

### 4. Goals Define Behavior
A system's purpose determines its behavior more than individual elements. Change the goal, change everything.
